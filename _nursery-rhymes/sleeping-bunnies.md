layout: nursery-rhyme
title: Sleeping bunnies
---

See the bunnies sleeping,<br/>
Till it's nearly noon.<br/>
Shall we wake them,<br/>
With a merry tune?<br/>
They're so still, are they ill?<br/>
Wake up soon.<br/>
Wake up bunnies!

Hop little bunnies, hop, hop, hop.<br/>
Hop little bunnies, hop, hop, hop.<br/>
Hop little bunnies, hop, hop, hop.<br/>
Hope, hop, hop.
