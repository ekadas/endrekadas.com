title: Bouncing baby
---

What shall we do with the bouncing baby?<br/>
What shall we do with the bouncing baby?<br/>
What shall we do with the bouncing baby?<br/>
Early in the morning.

Let's tickle, tickle, tickle, tickle their tummy,<br/>
Tickle, tickle, tickle, tickle their tummy,<br/>
Tickle, tickle, tickle, tickle their tummy,<br/>
Early in the morning.

Let's stroke, stroke, stroke their legs,<br/>
Stroke, stroke, stroke their legs,<br/>
Stroke, stroke, stroke their legs,<br/>
Early in the morning.

Let's wiggle, wiggle, wiggle their fingers,<br/>
Wiggle, wiggle, wiggle their fingers,<br/>
Wiggle, wiggle, wiggle their fingers,<br/>
Early in the morning.
