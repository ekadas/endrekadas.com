title: Open shut them
---

Open shut them – open shut them,<br/>
Give a little clap.<br/>
Open shut them – open shut them,<br/>
Lay them in your lap.<br/>

Open shut them – open shut them,<br/>
Give a little shake.<br/>
Open shut them – open shut them,<br/>
Keep them wide awake.<br/>

Open shut them – open shut them,<br/>
Roll them up like this.<br/>
Open shut them – open shut them,<br/>
Blow a little kiss<br/>

Open shut them – open shut them,<br/>
Don't get in a muddle.<br/>
Open shut them – open shut them,<br/>
Give yourself a cuddle.<br/>
